Subscription & billing

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, so you cancel from your iPhone's settings. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

  1. 1Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. 2Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. 3Choose Notebble and tap Cancel Subscription.
Settings› Your name› Subscriptions› Notebble

Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've already paid for. You can also get there from the app: Settings → Manage subscriptions → Manage subscription.

How do I restore my purchases?

Reinstalled Notebble or moved to a new iPhone? Your Pro subscription is tied to your Apple ID, so you can restore it in a few taps.

  1. 1Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.
  2. 2Open Notebble and tap the gear icon in the top-right corner.
  3. 3Tap Manage subscriptions, then Restore at the top of the screen.

Restoring brings back your Pro access, not your notes. Notes and reminders are stored only on the iPhone where you created them.

How do I request a refund?

All payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple directly — we aren't able to issue them ourselves.

  1. 1Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.
  2. 2Tap I'd like to and choose Request a refund.
  3. 3Pick a reason, select your Notebble purchase and submit.
Open reportaproblem.apple.com

What's included in Pro?

Pro removes every limit of the free plan. It's a monthly, auto-renewing subscription — the current price is shown in the app before you buy.

Feature Free Pro
Notes 3 Unlimited
Reminders 4 Unlimited
Freeform page length 250 characters Unlimited
Note description length 120 characters Unlimited
Reminder description length 80 characters Unlimited
Edits per note or reminder 3 Unlimited

If you cancel, nothing is deleted — you keep everything you've written and simply can't go past the free limits again.

Using Notebble

Can I use Notebble for medication or medical reminders?

No. Notebble is a general productivity tool and is not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, monitor, or manage any medical condition, or to manage medication.

Don't use Notebble for medication schedules, medical appointments, or anything else where a missed, late, or incorrect reminder — or lost or inaccurate notes — could cause harm to you or others. Reminders are delivered by iOS and can be delayed or not shown at all (for example because of Focus modes, notification settings, or a device restart).

Keep an independent way to remember anything health-related, time-critical, or important. We also recommend not storing health data or other sensitive information in the app. See the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy.

My reminders aren't showing up

Check that notifications are allowed in Settings → Notifications → Notebble. Then open Notebble and pull down on the Reminders list — this re-registers every reminder with iOS.

Where are my notes stored? Can I sync them?

Everything is stored only on your iPhone. There's no account and no cloud sync, so we can't see or recover your notes. Deleting the app also deletes its notes and reminders.

Can I switch between freeform and structured notes?

Yes — go to Settings → Note-taking style. Switching doesn't delete anything: each style keeps its own content and it's there when you switch back.
